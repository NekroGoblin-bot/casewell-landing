THE PROBLEM

You know case studies sell. That's why the doc is still open in a tab.

Half-written case studies are one of the most common open tabs in an agency owner's browser. Not because the work wasn't good — because turning a messy conversation into something a prospect will actually read takes hours you don't have.

01 — TIME

Three hours per case study, minimum

Transcribing the call, finding the narrative, writing it up, formatting it — by the time it's done, you've moved on to the next client.

02 — CONSISTENCY

Whoever had time that week wrote it. Structure, tone, and quality swing wildly across your site.

QUESTIONS

Before you ask

Do I still need to interview my client?

Yes — for now. You (or whoever manages the account) answers the questions based on the conversation you already had. A client-facing version where they answer directly is on the roadmap.

Will it make up results that aren't true?

No. It only works with the numbers and details you give it — it won't invent metrics. You review and edit everything before it goes anywhere.

What will it cost?

Free for your first case study. Paid plans start around $29/month for unlimited case studies, PDF export, and your own branding — pricing may shift slightly based on early access feedback.
